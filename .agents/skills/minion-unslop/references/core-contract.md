# Advisory cleanup contract

## Goal

Identify concrete AI-writing or clarity defects while preserving meaning. Prefer a protected match or no-op to an uncertain edit.

## Findings

Quote the smallest span and explain the contextual defect: formulaic framing, empty abstraction, unsupported certainty, opaque or mixed metaphor, misleading heading, repetition, contradiction, or ambiguity. Track hard matches as confirmed or protected with a reason.

Do not infer truth from outside knowledge. Missing proof alone does not make a plan, future date, promotion, technical term, literal phrase, or attribution defective. A scanner match alone never authorizes an edit. Protect quoted, attributed, domain-valid, literal, accurately caveated, and genre-natural uses. Soft cadence and silhouette scores require corroboration.

## Report-only default

Report findings and protections without changing files or source text. Findings never determine merge, publication, approval, or acceptance. Non-English prose is outside scope.

## Rewrite only on explicit request

Edit only confirmed spans with the smallest repair. Copy every other sentence byte-for-byte in its original order and paragraph. Add no claims, advice, anecdote, certainty, or conclusion. Preserve facts, quantities, dates, names, quotations, citations, code, commands, units, scope, uncertainty, attribution, register, and meaning. Preserve `never`, `must`, `only`, and `all` exactly in safety, security, legal, policy, and technical rules.

## Decision rule

Return a finding only when the contextual defect is clearer than the preservation risk. Treat deterministic output as evidence for review, not a verdict.
