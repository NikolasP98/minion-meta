What needs to change first

1 · Reliable writes

Use shared error handling. Reject stale writes atomically. An old save response must never clear a newer draft.

2 · Propagate commits

Map entity changes to page dependencies, query keys and local caches. Extend the existing private org channel and recover after missed signals.

3 · Consolidate invariants

Unify party validation, recipe resolution and tagging contracts. Extend shared UI where variants differ, then migrate callers.

The Notion-style editing contract

Autosave ordinary properties

Names, reversible scalar values and manual tags commit on blur, Enter, selection or bounded debounce. Show pending, saved, error and conflict states.

Keep explicit commitments

Multi-field forms, payments, invoice issuance, stock posting, deletion and cancellation keep submit or confirmation actions.

Verify across representations

Two views, two tabs and two authorized users converge on one committed version. A foreign organization receives no record details. Reconnect repairs missed changes.

Full source index

Preserve deliberate differences

Invoice ingestion and emission are different operations. Contact auto-tags retain their own metadata. SvelteKit business loads, gateway push state, client query cache and server cache have different owners. Statistical control bands, stock illustrations and accessibility tables are legitimate specialized renderers.
