Where Save disappears

Autosave ordinary edits

Names, internal notes, tags, harmless preferences, table cells, personal layouts, draft edits and ordinary booking moves.

Commit on blur, Enter, selection, drop or bounded debounce. Require version and reconciliation support.

Keep coherent forms

Booking creation, interdependent units/packaging, multi-field rule configuration and other coupled validation.

Immediate local previews; one deliberate Create or Apply.

Keep consequential commands

Charge, issue invoice, publish, send, grant access, post stock, cancel with side effects and close shift.

Immediate pending feedback; effective success follows server confirmation.

Motion communicates state

Instant · 75 ms

Optional press response. Intent changes on input, without a network wait.

Fast · 150 ms

Toggle, chip, status and result transitions. Cosmetic spinner appears only for sustained pending work.

Normal · 250 ms

Scoped rollback, drawer entry and confirmed chart deltas. Never block work on animation.

Reduced motion removes movement and delays while preserving pending, errors, focus and every action. One coalesced live-region owner announces status per editing context.
