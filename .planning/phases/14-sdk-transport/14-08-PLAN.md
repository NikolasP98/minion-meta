<selected_contract>
Add to existing GatewayClientOptions only:
```ts
onAuthenticated?: (
  hello: unknown,
  session: { readonly generation: number },
) => void | Promise<void>;
```
The generation is the production counter already advanced by connect(), paired internally with the actual socket. No test-only generation or Date.now identity. Emit once after the correlated connect response succeeds for that still-owned attempt, including initial, automatic and backup connections. Socket open/challenge/failure/duplicate/stale completion does not emit. Keep unknown payload typing: this callback is transport acceptance, not a new cryptographic identity or runtime HelloOk validation claim. 14-01 owns the broader envelope/receiver contract; do not silently introduce incompatible wire validation here.

Each attempt owns its socket, handshake completion callbacks and timers. Recheck ownership after awaiting challenge and hello and before sending, settling, notifying or error-close. Explicit close/supersession invalidates handshake work synchronously; it must not depend on the eventual native close event. Retain necessary onClose delivery to consumers and never let an old socket callback mutate newer state. Superseded pending connect promises and timers must settle/clean up rather than remaining orphaned. A duplicate challenge received while the first challenge callback is pending must neither invoke that callback again nor send a second connect request; repeated challenge after success is also inert for that attempt.

Resolve and clear the successful attempt's promise bookkeeping before observer invocation, in the same continuation before caller then microtasks. Observer code can close or initiate a successor without corrupting old bookkeeping. Do not await observer hydration. Both synchronous throws and returned rejection are contained, do not change connect success or reconnect scheduling, and produce one fixed nonpayload diagnostic: `[GatewayClient] onAuthenticated observer failed`. Do not include exception values, hello, frame, credentials, URL or token; do not send observer failures to any of the three other hooks. Diagnostic reporting itself must not escape if console.error throws.
</selected_contract>
